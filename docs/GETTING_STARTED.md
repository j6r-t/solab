# Sofien Optic — Getting Started

Welcome to **Sofien Optic**, the program that runs your optics shop and your workshop (atelier). It keeps your orders, prescriptions, clients, stock, cheques and repairs in one place, so nothing lives on paper anymore. It works in French and English, in the shop, in the atelier, and on your phone.

---

## What you gain

- **No more lost paper receipts.** Every sale and every payment is saved, with a printable facture or reçu.
- **You always know who owes you.** Unpaid orders, pending cheques and traites are counted for you, at any second.
- **Stock never surprises you.** You see low items before they run out, and dead items that block your money.
- **The atelier workload is visible.** Pending repairs and work orders, with ages, on one screen.
- **Decisions backed by numbers.** Revenue, profit, top clients, money waiting outside — computed for you.

To sign in:

1. Open the app. The login page appears.
2. Type your email and password.

To sign out, click the **Déconnexion** button. To change your password, open **Paramètres** (Settings) → **Profil**.

## Your first 10-minute tour

Do this once, with the owner account, to see the whole flow.

1. **Look at the dashboard.** Open **Tableau de bord**. You see your key numbers (revenue, clients, today's orders, low stock, pending repairs) and the **Prêt à récupérer** list: orders ready to hand to clients.
2. **Create a client.** Open **Clients** → **Nouveau client**. Fill in name and phone, then **Enregistrer**.
3. **Create a prescription.** Open **Ordonnances** → **Nouvelle ordonnance**. Pick the client, the doctor, and enter SPH / CYL / AXE for each eye. Save.
4. **Create an order.** Open **Commandes** → **Nouvelle commande**. Pick the client. The latest prescription is selected automatically. Add a frame from Stock, add the lenses, and add a repair service if needed (for example remontage).
5. **Take the payment.** Cash (**Espèces**) and card (**Carte**) count as paid immediately. For a cheque (**Chèque**) or traite (**Traite**), enter the number, the bank, and the due date (**échéance**). You can combine several payment rows, for example cash plus two cheques.
6. **Print the facture.** When you save the order, the app offers to print the **FACTURE**. Click **Imprimer**. You can print it again later from **Facturation**.
7. **Follow the order.** When the glasses are assembled, open the order and click **Marquer comme prêt**. When the client picks them up, click **Marquer comme récupéré**.
8. **Cash the cheques.** Later, when a cheque comes back from the bank, open **Chèques** and click **Encaisser**. Only then does the money count as received.

That is the whole loop: client → prescription → order → payment → facture → pickup → cheque cashed.

## Your first week: daily routine

### Every morning

- [ ] Open **Tableau de bord** and read the KPI cards.
- [ ] Click the bell (notifications). Check: cheques due soon (**Chèques à échoir**), low stock (**Stock bas**), pending repairs (**Réparations en attente**), orders ready (**Commandes prêtes**).
- [ ] Call clients in the **Prêt à récupérer** list.

### During the day

- [ ] Serve each client with **Nouvelle commande** (or **Vente rapide** in **Facturation** for small sales).
- [ ] When glasses are ready, click **Marquer comme prêt**.
- [ ] Record every payment in the app, never on paper.

### Every evening

- [ ] Open **Chèques**. Cheques or traites that came back from the bank today: click **Encaisser**. This is what turns paper into money in your reports.
- [ ] Open **Rapports** → **Créances**. This is your money outside: unpaid orders, cheques waiting, cheques overdue. Make one call if needed.
- [ ] Check the low-stock card and note what to order from your suppliers.

## Where to get help

Two documents come with the app, in the `docs` folder:

- **USER_MANUAL.md** — step-by-step instructions for every task, with the exact button names.
- **PRODUCT_GUIDE.md** — what each part of the app does and why it helps you.

If a screen name in this guide does not match what you see, check that the language is set to what you expect in **Paramètres** → **Langue**.

## Check the shop from your phone

The app can be opened from anywhere through a secure internet link (already configured with a tunnel). Ask whoever set up the app for the link, open it in your phone browser, and sign in as usual. Useful to check the dashboard and the day's numbers without going to the shop.
